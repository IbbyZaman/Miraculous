MiraculousHub version 4 — custom domains

This update uses the selected version-4 ZIP as its base.

Website video/subtitle/download/party endpoint: https://video.miraculoushub.co.uk
Website uploader endpoint: https://upload.miraculoushub.co.uk
The video and uploader Wrangler configs disable workers.dev and retain their
existing Worker names and bindings. Their custom-domain routes are included.

DEPLOY
1. In Cloudflare, replace miraculous-video code with cloudflare-worker-current.js
   and deploy. Retain R2_VIDEOS, WATCH_PARTIES, all migrations and secrets.
2. The uploader code is unchanged. Retain VIDEOS, UPLOAD_PASSWORD and AUTO_SUBS.
   On the uploader and subtitle workflow Workers, set VIDEO_WORKER_ORIGIN to
   https://video.miraculoushub.co.uk. Redeploy those Workers if required.
3. Upload the included website files to your existing GitHub Pages website.
   site-config.js now holds the new domains and its HTML cache version changed.
4. Keep the custom domains active and the old video/uploader workers.dev routes
   disabled. If deploying with Wrangler, fill in the existing bucket/account
   placeholders or merge these settings into your production configs; retain
   all existing bindings, secrets and migration history.

BEHAVIOUR
Visiting the video domain root redirects to https://miraculoushub.co.uk/.
Direct address-bar visits to video/subtitle/party links also redirect.
Video, subtitles and downloads are allowed only when Origin/Referer identifies
https://miraculoushub.co.uk or its www address. Foreign browser embeds and
requests without site headers are refused. Downloads clicked on the website
require sign-in and a temporary authorized link. Watch Together keeps its site-only origin policy.
Signed internal auto-subtitle ingestion remains supported.
Read ACCOUNT-DOWNLOADS-AND-CLOUD.txt for the sign-in download update.

CHECK LIVE
Open an uploaded episode through miraculoushub.co.uk: play and seek, change
subtitles, download, and create/join a Watch Together room on two devices.
Upload a small test episode and confirm its subtitles job can fetch the video.
Open the video domain and a copied video link directly: both should redirect.
Try a foreign-site browser embed: it should be denied.

These are browser hotlink controls, not DRM. A client can imitate site headers
or save an episode it can play. Keep your R2 bucket private and avoid separate
public bucket URLs which bypass this Worker.

Player appearance, Firebase progress, episode data and thumbnails are retained.
This ZIP prepares the change; it does not deploy anything to your accounts.
