CLOUDFLARE SETUP

1. Replace the Worker code with worker.js.
2. Deploy the Worker.
3. Keep GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT as an encrypted Worker secret; do not paste it into the website.
4. Share the three Google Drive files with the service account as Viewer.

Routes:
/video
/video/bubbler
/video/pharaoh
/download/stormy-weather
/download/bubbler
/download/pharaoh

The website uses a hidden iframe for downloads so clicking Download Episode does not navigate the current page.

Note: large downloads are subject to browser, Cloudflare Worker, and Google Drive transfer limits.
