OWNER CONTENT TOOLS

1. Deploy cloudflare-uploader-worker.js to the existing upload.miraculoushub.co.uk Worker. Keep its VIDEOS R2 binding and existing UPLOAD_PASSWORD secret. No new bindings or Firestore rules are required. The video Worker stays as it is.
2. Upload this full website to GitHub, including catalog.js, upload-admin.js and trailers.js.
3. Open https://miraculoushub.co.uk/upload.html and sign in as ibbyzaman4@gmail.com. Use Continue with Google with that account, or an email/password account whose email has been verified. Enter the existing upload password.
4. For a trailer choose Trailer and Season 7, give it a title and a unique lowercase ID such as season-7-trailer-2, optionally supply a HTTPS thumbnail, and upload the MP4. It appears on the Season 7 page automatically. Reusing an ID replaces that trailer.
5. For an episode choose its season and episode and upload the MP4 first. Then use Add or update an episode card to publish its title, thumbnail and NEW badge. The home page, Episodes page, search and player load saved cards on each visit. Existing season/episode numbers update existing cards.

Cards are stored as individual catalog JSON objects in the existing VIDEOS bucket. The bucket must remain private. If you upload via the Cloudflare dashboard manually, use the same destination shown by the uploader before publishing its card. The catalog never accepts arbitrary video URLs.

Only the server-verified, enabled, verified-email owner account can publish cards or upload trailers. Existing password-protected episode/special/anime uploads remain supported. Passwords and ID tokens are never stored in the public catalog. Public cards load once per visit; existing built-in content remains available when the catalog cannot be reached. The supplied Season 7 teaser remains available alongside newly uploaded trailers.
