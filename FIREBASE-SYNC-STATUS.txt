Firebase cross-device playback sync is wired in this version.

Requirements:
1. Firebase Authentication must have the desired sign-in providers enabled.
2. Firestore must be created and the included firestore.rules deployed.
3. The same Firebase account must be used on both devices.

The player keeps localStorage as a fast cache, saves playback to users/{uid}/playback/{seasonEpisode}, loads the cloud position when signed in, and refreshes Continue Watching after cloud sync.
