The 95/96 player IDs are internal catalog slots, not official episode numbers.
The website and uploader display Special 1/Special 2 instead.
Do not upload a complete long-form special under e88/e89; those keys are
reserved for the split episodic version. Specials and episodes 88–94 contain
the same finale story in different cuts.

Episode cards, uploader choices, search and Continue Watching all use the
same anime-data.js catalog. There are no external episode download links
or embedded video files in this package; upload your matching MP4s to R2.

All titles, season numbers and exact upload/watch/download paths are in
attack-on-titan-upload-manifest.json. Titles use the short English names;
Season 1's repeating story-arc subtitles are omitted for readable cards.
