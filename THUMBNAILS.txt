MiraculousHub thumbnail pack
-----------------------------
This build includes an original, non-show-still thumbnail for every listed episode and special.
Files are SVG and are loaded automatically by the episode cards, homepage latest episodes,
Continue Watching cards, and the watch player poster.

To replace a thumbnail with an image you have permission to use, keep the same filename
(e.g. S06E25.svg) or update the corresponding thumbnail path in the HTML.
