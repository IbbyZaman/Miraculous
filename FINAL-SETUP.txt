Firebase:
1. Enable Email/Password and Google sign-in in Firebase Authentication.
2. Create Firestore.
3. Apply firestore.rules.
4. Add your deployed website domain to Firebase Authentication Authorized domains.

Cloudflare:
Use the included worker.js and keep GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT as a Worker secret.

Favicon:
favicon.png should be in the same directory as index.html, episodes.html and watch.html.
